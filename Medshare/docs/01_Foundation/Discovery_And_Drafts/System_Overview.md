# 🏥 MedShare: Federated Learning Clinical Dashboard
### **Master Technical Demonstrator & User Guide (MEng Final Year Project Edition)**

The **MedShare Dashboard** is a high-fidelity interactive demonstrator designed to visualize the entire lifecycle of a **Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning (FL)** study. It bridges the gap between complex cryptographic training and real-world clinical governance.

---

## **🎯 I. Why This Dashboard Context Matters**
In traditional medical research, data is pooled centrally, creating **massive privacy risks.** 
**The MedShare Solution:** This dashboard proves that we can train high-accuracy models (e.g., our **80.1% Accuracy Gold Standard Run**) without the data ever leaving the hospital’s firewall.

### **Core Pillars:**
1.  **Data Sovereignty**: Prove that hospitals maintain 100% control of their records.
2.  **Economic Incentive**: Use the **Ethereum Bounty System** to reward participation.
3.  **Auditable Science**: Provide real-time accuracy and "Epsilon" (Privacy Leakage) metrics to satisfy clinical auditors.

## **🎯 III. THE USER JOURNEYS (Step-by-Step)**

### **A. Researcher Creation (Researcher Portal)**
1.  **Data Topic**: Selects the specific dataset requirement (e.g., "Diabetes Study").
2.  **Hospital Requirement**: Specifies the minimum number of medical centers (nodes) needed.
3.  **Model Selection**: Chooses the architecture (e.g., **MLP**, **CNN**, or **XGBoost**).
4.  **Incentivization (The Bounty)**: 
    *   The researcher inputs a **Total ETH Bounty** (e.g., 15 ETH).
    *   The system automatically splits this bounty among successful nodes.
    *   **Logic**: Each hospital receives `Total Bounty / Hospitals Needed` upon delivery.

### **B. Hospital Participation & Security**
1.  **Secure Linking**:
    *   The hospital selects its **Local Dataset** (Diabetes, Stroke, Heart) from the dropdown.
    *   **Privacy Guarantee**: The dashboard performs a **local-only handshake.** The raw dataset **stays behind the hospital firewall** and is never uploaded.
2.  **Local Training (The Handshake)**:
    *   Triggered by the **"Link & Participate"** button.
    *   Observation: **"🧪 Training Securely..."** animation (1.5s delay).
3.  **Model Submission**: Only the resulting weights & accuracy are sent to the aggregator—patient records remain inaccessible to the researcher.

### **C. Audit & Asset Retrieval**
1.  **Historical Audit Switcher (VIEW AUDIT)**: The top navigation features an interactive diagnostic switchboard.:
    *   **Current FL Simulation (Live)**: This loads the raw data generated by the actual Python simulation script (`comparison_stats.json`). It proves the dashboard can inject live, newly computed metrics.
    *   **Historical "Platinum" Audits**: Training a 250k-row model takes time. This dropdown allows the operator to instantly flip the dashboard into "Gold Standard Mode," rendering pre-computed metrics from massive completed studies (e.g., Maternal Health Risk, Robust-MAD). It demonstrates scalability to examiners instantly.
    *   **Auditors can switch between old audit JSONs (Thyroid, Maternal Health) to verify historical integrity.**
2.  **Asset Portal**: Once 100% full, the researcher retrieves the **Aggregated Weights (.pth)** and the **38.9% Accuracy Ceremony.**

## **🎓 V. Architecture Decisions & Viva Defense**

### **1. What model type should Federated Learning (FL) use?**
Federated Learning is technically model-agnostic, but it is deeply optimized for **Neural Networks (Deep Learning)**. FL relies on Secure Aggregation algorithms (like `FedAvg` and `Robust-MAD`) which mathematically average identical matrices. Neural networks learn by tweaking matrices of weights (gradients), making their architecture perfectly compatible with FL's encrypted averaging logic.

### **2. Why I used this specific model type (MLP)**
The project's core datasets (Diabetes, Thyroid, Maternal Risk) consist of "Tabular Data" (structured rows and columns of vitals/clinical markers). A **Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)** is statistically the most robust and accurate deep learning tool for mining complex, non-linear relationships in tabular data without over-engineering.

### **3. Why I didn't use CNN, Linear Regression, or XGBoost**
Choosing an MLP was an intentional exclusion of incompatible model types based on the project's Privacy-First scope:
*   **CNN (Convolutional Network):** CNNs are designed exclusively for spatial or image data (like X-Rays or MRIs). Attempting to slide a convolutional filter over an Excel spreadsheet of numerical clinical features is an architectural mismatch.
*   **Linear Regression:** Human clinical data is highly complex and non-linear. Simple linear mapping cannot capture the hidden relationships between age, weight, and blood sugar interacting synchronously.
*   **XGBoost:** XGBoost builds "Decision Trees." Building global trees across federated hospitals requires transmitting the split-criteria (e.g., "Age > 55"), which directly leaks private patient data and shatters Differential Privacy guarantees. Gradient-descent models (like MLPs) preserve privacy bounds natively. **XGBoost (Federated)** is included as a privacy-hardened variant of the algorithm, but remains architecturally secondary to Differential Privacy-enabled **MLPs** for medical data.
<br>

### **4. Why the UI has options for different model types**
The Smart Contract Marketplace is a **"Scalability Demonstrator."** While the backend mathematical proof focuses on an **MLP**, the Blockchain architecture is designed to govern *any* type of clinical research request. By exposing **CNN** and **XGBoost (Federated)** options in the UI, the project proves to stakeholders that the Ethereum Smart Contract infrastructure is **model-agnostic** and instantly ready to support future Image/Genomic datasets added to the hospital network.

### **5. Security Handshake (Schema Consistency Check)**
To prevent **Model Poisoning** (accidental or malicious), the project implements a **"Smart Handshake"** at the point of participation. 
*   **The Check**: Before a hospital can join a study, the system performs a string-similarity check between the Research Request's `dataType` and the Hospital's `linkedDataset`.
*   **The Result**: If a hospital selects "Stroke" data for a "Diabetes" study, the system instantly **REJECTS** the contribution with a schema mismatch alert. This proves to auditors that the system enforces **Domain Integrity** before any blockchain resources are consumed.

### **Defending the Scope (Viva Q&A)**

**Q: "Why didn't you test 5 different models simultaneously to see which had the best accuracy?"**
**A:** "This was a **System Engineering** project focused on securing Federated Networks. By keeping the model variable fixed to an MLP (the optimal architecture for Federated Tabular gradients), I was able to scientifically isolate and prove the mathematical effectiveness of my Differential Privacy bounds and Blockchain Reputation logic. Testing 5 different models would have 'muddied the variables,' making it impossible to attribute security gains solely to my MedShare Aggregation algorithms."

**Q: "What exactly does the 'Model Performance Comparison' chart on the dashboard prove?"**
**A:** "The chart provides the **visual mathematical proof** of the incentive to participate in Federated Learning. 
*   **The Dotted Grey Line (Local AUC):** Represents the highest predictive power each hospital can achieve if they selfishly train an AI *only* on their own siloed, imbalanced patient data. It fluctuates wildly across hospitals due to varying sample sizes and isolated bias.
*   **The Solid Purple Line (Federated AUC):** Represents the performance of the single, unified Global Model. It is drawn as a flat threshold across the top of the chart to serve as a global performance ceiling.
The vast vertical gap between the purple line and the grey line mathematically demonstrates the fundamental purpose of the project: it proves that every single isolated hospital receives a dramatically smarter, more accurate AI by collaborating securely via the MedShare Smart Contract than they could ever build alone."

**Q: "Why does the Federated Training Progress chart have a 'Loss' legend entry but no Loss line for some datasets (e.g. Maternal Health, CDC Diabetes)?"**
**A:** "The Loss metric was not captured during those federated training runs. The Python logging pipeline was scoped to record the primary scientific outputs per round: **accuracy**, **epsilon** (privacy budget), and **leakage** metrics. These were the critical values needed to validate the Privacy-Utility Tradeoff hypothesis. Per-round training loss was not part of the experimental hypothesis being tested for those datasets. The chart template always reserves a legend slot for Loss to maintain visual consistency across all audit views — for datasets where it was not logged (Maternal Health, CDC Diabetes, Admin), the line simply has no data points and is therefore invisible. The only dataset where both Accuracy and Loss were fully captured is the Thyroid run, which exported a complete round-by-round `training_history.json` from the Python simulation."

## **🛡️ VI. System Robustness & Fail-Safe Logic**

### 1. ⛓️ Blockchain High-Availability (Dynamic Monitoring)
The MedShare backend is designed to be **port-agnostic**. It automatically monitors connection attempts on **Port 8545** (Standard Ganache) and **Port 8546** (Warp). If no active blockchain is detected, the platform enters an **Authenticated Demo Mode**, allowing for presentation even in offline environments.

### 2. 🛡️ Decentralized Wallet Mapping (Security Guard)
A **Strict Index Guard** handles the mapping of hospital node indices to Ganache accounts. The system reserves account `0` for the Researcher/Admin and strictly tiles hospital wallets starting from index `1`. Attempting to simulate more hospitals than available wallets will result in a clean initialization error rather than a silent corruption.

### 3. 📊 Scientific Data Balancing (SMOTE)
To ensure model accuracy on skewed clinical datasets (e.g., mortality prediction), the `medshare/data.py` layer applies SMOTE to training partitions. This is a memory-intensive operation; for datasets exceeding 1,000,000 rows, users are advised to increase system swap or disable rebalancing in the experimental config.

### 4. 🔗 Frontend Fail-Safe (Persistent Storage)
The hospital portal uses an **Auto-Fallback sync logic**. If the `deploy_info.json` from the hardhat build is missing or unreadable, the marketplace will automatically default to a **LocalState persistent view**. This ensures the UI remains interactive and demonstratable even without a functioning blockchain backend.

## **🛡️ VII. Secure Aggregation & Pairwise Masking**

While **Differential Privacy (DP)** protects the *data* within the model, **Secure Aggregation (SecAgg)** protects the *transmission* of the model itself. MedShare implements a "Pairwise Masking" protocol to ensure the central researcher is mathematically blinded to individual hospital updates.

> [!NOTE]
> **Academic Disclosure (SecAgg Scope)**: For the purpose of this MEng demonstrator, the SecAgg masks in `medshare/utils.py` are generated using a centrally-seeded pseudo-random simulation. While this functionally demonstrates the mathematical cancellation of noise within the global aggregate, a production-level deployment would require a decentralized Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange (DHKE) setup to achieve true cryptographic security.

### **The "Secret Add" Cryptographic Logic:**
To prevent a researcher from performing a **Gradient Inversion Attack** (reconstructing patient records from a hospital's raw weights), MedShare uses symmetric noise cancellation:

1.  **Shared Secret**: Two hospitals (Node A and Node B) agree on a secret seed using a masked handshake.
2.  **The Masking Step**: 
    *   **Hospital A** adds a random mask ($M$) to its model weights before sending: $(W_A + M)$
    *   **Hospital B** subtracts the same mask ($M$) from its model weights: $(W_B - M)$
3.  **The Aggregation Step**: When the central researcher adds them together:
    $$(W_A + M) + (W_B - M) = W_A + W_B$$
    **The masks vanish!** The researcher calculates a 100% accurate global average without ever seeing the "true" weights of either hospital.

### **Viva Defense Logic (The "Researcher-Blind" Proof):**
*   **Q: "If you have Differential Privacy, why do you need Pairwise Masking?"**
*   **A:** "Differential Privacy protects the final model from leaking data after training. However, without **Pairwise Masking**, a malicious researcher could intercept the 'raw' update from a single hospital and attempt to reverse-engineer the gradients. Pairwise Masking ensures that the individual updates remain cryptographically 'gibberish' until they are safely combined into the global average."
